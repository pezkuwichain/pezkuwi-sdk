<h2>2. Introduction</h2>
<p>The advent of blockchain technology has presented unprecedented opportunities to build decentralized, transparent, and secure digital infrastructures. However, most existing blockchain solutions are designed as general-purpose platforms, often falling short of addressing the specific cultural, economic, and governance needs of globally dispersed, yet culturally unified nations.</p>
<p>PezkuwiChain emerges with the vision of creating a dedicated digital state for the Kurdish nation, leveraging the power of blockchain to address deep-rooted issues and lay the foundation for a prosperous digital future. PezkuwiChain is not merely a cryptocurrency; it is a comprehensive ecosystem designed to provide the tools for digital sovereignty.</p>
<p>Its mission is to serve the Kurdish people by providing a secure and decentralized platform for financial services, digital identity, democratic governance, and education. This document details PezkuwiChain's technical and economic architecture, its innovative solutions, and the strategic vision that will ensure its adoption and long-term success.</p>

<h2>3. The Problem</h2>
<p>Traditional financial and administrative systems often present significant barriers to entry, lack transparency, and are poorly adapted to the unique needs of nations that are globally dispersed yet culturally unified. Tens of millions of Kurdish people face distinct challenges that a sovereign digital infrastructure can address:</p>
<p><strong>Financial Exclusion:</strong> A significant portion of the population lacks access to modern banking and financial services, hindering economic growth and individual prosperity.</p>
<p><strong>Lack of Digital Sovereignty:</strong> The absence of a unified, sovereign digital identity system complicates citizen participation, access to services, and cross-border legal recognition.</p>
<p><strong>Governance Deficits:</strong> Centralized governance models can be opaque and lack mechanisms for broad, democratic participation, failing to capture the collective will of the people.</p>
<p><strong>Economic Volatility:</strong> National economies are often vulnerable to the volatility of external currencies and geopolitical pressures. A local digital currency can provide a more stable and controlled economic environment.</p>
<p><strong>Trust Deficit in Blockchain:</strong> Existing blockchain consensus mechanisms fail to incorporate social trust and reputation, which are fundamental to building resilient and community-driven networks. Pure economic incentives can lead to centralization and misaligned behavior.</p>

<h2>4. The Solution: PezkuwiChain Architecture (Multi-Chain Updated)</h2>
<p>PezkuwiChain is architecturally designed as a comprehensive solution to these challenges, providing a secure, decentralized, and sovereign digital backbone for the Kurdish nation. It is built using the Polkadot SDK, the state-of-the-art framework for creating next-generation blockchains.</p>
<h3>4.1. Foundation: Polkadot SDK and Substrate</h3>
<p>The choice of Polkadot SDK provides PezkuwiChain with a robust, modular, and future-proof foundation. The core of this architecture is Substrate, which separates the blockchain's core logic (Runtime) from its client-side functionalities (Client).</p>
<p><strong>Runtime (State Transition Function):</strong> The heart of the blockchain, containing the business logic. It is compiled as WebAssembly (Wasm) for forkless, on-chain upgrades. This means the network can evolve and adapt without disruptive hard forks.</p>
<p><strong>Client:</strong> The native binary that manages network communication (via Libp2p), consensus (BABE and GRANDPA), and transaction handling. It acts as the host that executes the Wasm runtime.</p>
<p>This modular design, facilitated by the Framework for Runtime Aggregation of Modularized Entities (FRAME), allows for the seamless integration of pre-built modules (pallets) and the development of custom logic tailored to PezkuwiChain's specific needs.</p>
<h3>4.2. Consensus Innovation: Trust-Enhanced Nominated Proof-of-Stake (TNPoS)</h3>
<p>PezkuwiChain introduces a groundbreaking enhancement to the traditional Nominated Proof-of-Stake (NPoS) consensus mechanism by directly integrating a Trust System into the validator selection and reward distribution process. This novel approach, termed TNPoS (Trust-Enhanced Nominated Proof-of-Stake), combines the economic security of NPoS with a layer of social reputation provided by the custom <code>pallet-trust</code>.</p>
<h3>Figure 1: TNPoS Consensus Flow - Integration of Trust Scores into Validator Selection and Rewards</h3>
<p><img alt="TNPoS Consensus Flow" src="/home/ubuntu/FINAL_DESIGNS_RECOVERED/tnpos_consensus_flow.png" /></p>
<h3>4.2.1. Core Components of TNPoS</h3>
<p><strong>Traditional NPoS Foundation:</strong> TNPoS is based on Polkadot's proven NPoS model, where Validators are responsible for producing blocks and finalizing the chain, while Nominators stake their tokens to back and elect trusted Validators. This system democratizes participation in network security and ensures a high level of economic security.</p>
<p><strong>Trust Score Integration:</strong> <code>pallet-trust</code> introduces a social trust and reputation system, allowing network participants to build trust scores based on their behavior, contributions, and interactions within the ecosystem. These trust scores are not merely cosmetic; they are integrated into the consensus layer via the <code>pallet-pez-rewards</code> mechanism.</p>
<p><strong>Trust-Weighted Validator Selection:</strong> In TNPoS, validator selection is not solely based on the amount staked but is also influenced by the trust scores of both the Validators and their Nominators. This ensures that Validators with higher community trust and a proven track record are more likely to be elected, enhancing the overall reliability and security of the network.</p>
<p><strong>Trust-Based Reward Distribution:</strong> <code>pallet-pez-rewards</code> uses trust scores to calculate and distribute rewards. Validators and Nominators with higher trust scores receive proportionally higher rewards, creating a positive feedback loop that incentivizes good behavior, long-term commitment, and active participation in the network's governance and security.</p>
<h3>4.2.2. Block Production and Finality</h3>
<p>PezkuwiChain utilizes a hybrid consensus mechanism for block production and finality:</p>
<p><strong>BABE (Blind Assignment for Blockchain Extension):</strong> Responsible for block production. BABE is a slot-based protocol that randomly assigns block production rights to validators, ensuring censorship resistance and providing a consistent block time of approximately 6 seconds.</p>
<p><strong>GRANDPA (GHOST-based Recursive ANcestor Deriving Prefix Agreement):</strong> Responsible for finality. GRANDPA is a finality gadget that allows the chain to finalize large numbers of blocks quickly, providing near-instant confirmation of transactions.</p>
<h3>4.3. Multi-Chain Architecture</h3>
<p>PezkuwiChain is designed as a multi-chain ecosystem, leveraging the power of Polkadot's Parachain model for specialized functionality and scalability.</p>
<p><strong>1. Pezkuwi Relay Chain (Core):</strong>
*   <strong>Function:</strong> Provides shared security and finality for the entire ecosystem.
*   <strong>Consensus:</strong> TNPoS (HEZ token).
*   <strong>Pallets:</strong> Core system pallets (Balances, Staking, Session, etc.).</p>
<p><strong>2. Asset Hub Parachain:</strong>
*   <strong>Function:</strong> Dedicated to managing all native and foreign assets, including PEZ, HEZ, and other tokens.
*   <strong>Interoperability:</strong> Facilitates cross-chain transfers (XCM) between the Relay Chain and other Parachains.</p>
<p><strong>3. People Chain Parachain:</strong>
*   <strong>Function:</strong> Dedicated to identity, governance, and social interaction.
*   <strong>Pallets:</strong> <code>identity-kyc</code>, <code>welati</code>, <code>perwerde</code>, <code>trust</code>, <code>referral</code>, <code>tiki</code>.
*   <strong>Focus:</strong> High-throughput for social and identity transactions.</p>

<h2>5. Dual-Token Economic Model</h2>
<p>PezkuwiChain operates on a sophisticated dual-token economy, separating the concerns of network security and governance/utility.</p>
<h3>5.1. HEZ Token (Security Token)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Name:</strong> HEZ (Hez - Kurdish for "Strength")</li>
<li><strong>Role:</strong> Network security, staking, and validator rewards.</li>
<li><strong>Model:</strong> Inflationary.</li>
<li><strong>Supply:</strong> Uncapped, with a target annual inflation rate of ~10%.</li>
<li><strong>Distribution:</strong> 85% to Nominators/Validators, 15% to the Treasury.</li>
<li><strong>Utility:</strong> Staking for security, paying transaction fees on the Relay Chain.</li>
</ul>
<h3>5.2. PEZ Token (Utility &amp; Governance Token)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Name:</strong> PEZ (Pez - Kurdish for "Wealth/Treasure")</li>
<li><strong>Role:</strong> Governance, utility, rewards, and ecosystem growth.</li>
<li><strong>Model:</strong> Deflationary/Fixed Supply.</li>
<li><strong>Supply:</strong> Fixed at 5 Billion PEZ.</li>
<li><strong>Distribution:</strong> Initial distribution via presale, rewards, and treasury.</li>
<li><strong>Utility:</strong> Governance voting, paying for services on the People Chain (identity, education), rewards for trusted behavior, and treasury funding.</li>
</ul>

<h2>6. Core Features &amp; Custom Pallets</h2>
<h3>6.1. Digital Identity and Citizenship (<code>pallet-identity-kyc</code> &amp; <code>pallet-tiki</code>)</h3>
<p>PezkuwiChain provides a unified, sovereign digital identity system.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>KYC/AML Compliance:</strong> <code>pallet-identity-kyc</code> manages the verification status of users, ensuring compliance with global standards.</li>
<li><strong>Hemwelatî NFT:</strong> Upon successful KYC, users receive a non-transferable Hemwelatî (Citizen) NFT via <code>pallet-tiki</code>, serving as their digital citizenship card.</li>
<li><strong>Role-Based NFTs:</strong> <code>pallet-tiki</code> also manages higher-tier NFTs for elected officials (Parlementer, Wezir, Serok).</li>
</ul>
<h3>6.2. Democratic Governance (<code>pallet-welati</code>)</h3>
<p>The <code>pallet-welati</code> establishes a robust, on-chain democratic system.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>201-Member Parliament:</strong> A digital parliament of 201 elected representatives.</li>
<li><strong>On-Chain Voting:</strong> All legislative proposals and votes are recorded on the blockchain.</li>
<li><strong>PEZ Treasury:</strong> Managed by <code>pallet-pez-treasury</code>, funded by a portion of PEZ rewards and transaction fees.</li>
</ul>
<h3>6.3. Education and Certification (<code>pallet-perwerde</code>)</h3>
<p>The <code>pallet-perwerde</code> creates a verifiable education ecosystem.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Certificate Management:</strong> Institutions can issue blockchain-verified certificates.</li>
<li><strong>IPFS Storage:</strong> Certificates are stored on IPFS, with the hash recorded on-chain for permanent verification.</li>
</ul>
<h3>6.4. Trust and Rewards (<code>pallet-trust</code> &amp; <code>pallet-pez-rewards</code>)</h3>
<p>The core innovation of TNPoS is realized through these two pallets.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Trust Score Calculation:</strong> <code>pallet-trust</code> calculates a score based on:<ol>
<li>KYC Status</li>
<li>Staking History</li>
<li>Governance Participation</li>
<li>Referral Success</li>
<li>Ecosystem Contributions</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li><strong>Trust-Weighted Rewards:</strong> <code>pallet-pez-rewards</code> distributes PEZ rewards proportionally to the user's trust score, incentivizing positive community behavior.</li>
</ul>

<h2>9. Governance Model</h2>
<p>PezkuwiChain's governance is a hybrid system combining the efficiency of a representative democracy with the transparency of on-chain voting.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>The Parliament:</strong> 201 elected representatives (Parlementer NFT holders) vote on proposals.</li>
<li><strong>Elections:</strong> Managed by <code>pallet-welati</code>, with regular, transparent elections.</li>
<li><strong>Referenda:</strong> Community members can propose and vote on referenda using their PEZ tokens.</li>
</ul>

<h2>19. Appendix A: Glossary</h2>
<p><strong>BABE</strong>: Blind Assignment for Blockchain Extension - Block production mechanism.
<strong>Cumulus</strong>: Parachain development framework for Polkadot.
<strong>Era</strong>: Validator selection cycle (~1 day, 14,400 blocks).
<strong>Epoch</strong>: PEZ reward distribution cycle (~30 days, 432,000 blocks).
<strong>FRAME</strong>: Framework for Runtime Aggregation of Modularized Entities.
<strong>GRANDPA</strong>: GHOST-based Recursive ANcestor Deriving Prefix Agreement - Finality gadget.
<strong>HEZ</strong>: Security token (Hez - Strength).
<strong>NPoS</strong>: Nominated Proof-of-Stake.
<strong>PEZ</strong>: Utility/Governance token (Pez - Wealth/Treasure).
<strong>Substrate</strong>: Modular blockchain framework.
<strong>TNPoS</strong>: Trust-Enhanced Nominated Proof-of-Stake.
<strong>Wasm</strong>: WebAssembly - Used for forkless runtime upgrades.
<strong>Welati</strong>: Governance pallet (Kurdish for "Citizen/Homeland").
<strong>Perwerde</strong>: Education pallet (Kurdish for "Education").
<strong>Tiki</strong>: NFT pallet (Kurdish for "Unique/Single").</p>
